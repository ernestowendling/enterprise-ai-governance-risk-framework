# AI Risk Management Standard

## Purpose
Translate AI risk appetite into repeatable classification, assessment, treatment and acceptance.

## Requirements
Every use is classified with the approved rule version and documented rationale. Assessment covers strategic, conduct, model, data, privacy, security, operational, legal, third-party, explainability, fairness, oversight, reputational and GenAI risks where relevant. Owners assess inherent risk, evidence control effectiveness, determine residual risk and record findings. AI Governance challenges completeness; specialist second-line functions review their domains. High residual risk requires committee acceptance; critical residual risk requires executive acceptance or rejection.

Assessments are refreshed at the defined frequency and after material change, new use, degradation, drift, incident, regulatory change or provider-version change. Unsupported judgement or missing evidence blocks approval. Exceptions follow the policy exception process. Review cycle: annual.
