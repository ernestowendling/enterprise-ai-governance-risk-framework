# Model and AI Validation Standard

Independent validation establishes whether an AI system is fit for its intended use and whether limitations are understood and controlled. High and critical systems require validation by a competent party independent from development; moderate systems receive proportionate challenge.

Scope includes intended use, conceptual soundness, performance, stability, data quality, limitations, explainability, robustness, fairness, human interaction, security, third-party dependency and change impact. Findings are severity-rated and linked to approval conditions. Initial validation precedes approval. Periodic and event-driven revalidation respond to risk tier, material change, new use, degradation, drift, regulatory change, incident and provider-version change. Model Risk owns the standard and signed reports are retained as evidence. Review cycle: annual.
