# Human Oversight Standard

Human oversight must be meaningful, competent and operationally feasible. The design selects human-in-the-loop for consequential decisions, human-on-the-loop for supervised operation, or human-in-command for lower-autonomy uses.

The Business Owner defines who reviews, when intervention occurs, information available, override authority, stop authority, workload limits and escalation. Reviewers are trained and protected from automation bias. High-impact customer decisions require review before action and retain reviewer identity, timestamp, decision, explanation and override rationale. A missing or ineffective mandatory oversight control blocks approval. Effectiveness is tested through scenarios and decision-log samples. Review cycle: annual.
