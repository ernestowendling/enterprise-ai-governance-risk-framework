# Model Monitoring Standard

Production AI must have an approved monitoring plan connecting metrics and thresholds to accountable action. At minimum, relevant systems monitor performance, data and output drift, errors, exceptions, overrides, human review, fairness signals and incidents.

GREEN continues routine monitoring. AMBER creates an alert, investigation and potentially increased frequency. RED creates a finding, escalates to the risk owner, triggers revalidation and prompts a suspension decision. Owners must not average away material subgroup or customer harm. Technical Owners produce monitoring evidence; Business Owners interpret business impact; Model Risk and AI Governance challenge high-risk results. Thresholds require system-specific calibration. Review cycle: annual.
