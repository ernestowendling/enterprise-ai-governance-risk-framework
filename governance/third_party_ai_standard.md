# Third-Party AI Standard

Vendor AI remains subject to institutional accountability. Before contracting, the Business Owner and Third-Party Risk assess provider capability, model/version transparency, data flows, confidentiality, security, service availability, monitoring, change notification, subcontractors, concentration, validation limitations and exit.

Contracts must support evidence access, incident notice, material-change notification, data use restrictions and orderly exit where proportionate. Provider assurances supplement but do not replace institutional testing. Unnotified material change triggers reclassification and revalidation; unacceptable opacity may preclude use. Evidence includes due diligence, contract clauses, assurance reports, change logs and exit tests. Review cycle: annual.
