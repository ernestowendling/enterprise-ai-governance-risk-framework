# AI Inventory Standard

The central inventory is the authoritative population for AI governance, reporting and assurance. Business Owners register uses before procurement, experimentation with non-public data or development. Required attributes cover identity, purpose, ownership, technology, data, impact, jurisdiction, risk, lifecycle, validation, monitoring, findings, exceptions and retirement.

Technical Owners attest accuracy after material change and at least annually. AI Governance monitors completeness and reconciles the population against procurement, technology and model inventories. Duplicate IDs, invalid controlled values and missing required fields are rejected. Evidence comprises versioned submissions, approvals and change history. Unregistered or stale AI may be suspended. Review cycle: annual.
