# Five-Minute Demonstration: Loan Decision Support

1. **Executive Overview (0:00–0:30).** Establish a nine-system portfolio with proportionate tiers; point to critical systems, findings and vendor exposure.
2. **AI Inventory (0:30–0:55).** Select `AI-001`; show named owners, data, lifecycle, validation and next review.
3. **Risk Classification (0:55–1:30).** Select Loan Application Decision Support. Explain triggered factors, the CRITICAL tier, Executive Risk Committee approval and six-month revalidation.
4. **Risk Assessment (1:30–2:00).** Set Customer / Conduct risk to CRITICAL and controls to partially effective. Show HIGH residual risk and the resulting condition.
5. **Control Requirements (2:00–2:30).** Trace requirements to accountable roles and evidence, highlighting validation, fairness, explainability and oversight.
6. **Human Oversight (2:30–2:55).** Show human-in-the-loop review, override, stop authority and retained evidence.
7. **Lifecycle & Approval (2:55–3:35).** Untick validation or oversight to demonstrate a blocked approval; restore evidence to pass the gate.
8. **Monitoring (3:35–4:20).** Show deterioration to RED, PSI drift and the response: finding → escalation → revalidation → suspension consideration.
9. **Bias & Fairness (4:20–4:40).** Show the synthetic group-rate trigger and emphasise contextual judgement.
10. **Audit Trail (4:40–5:00).** Close with immutable-link evidence from intake through monitoring breach and revalidation.
