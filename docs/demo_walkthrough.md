# Five-Minute Connected Demonstration: Loan Decision Support

This journey uses one in-memory governance workspace. Every action affects later pages; no source files are edited.

1. **Executive Overview (0:00–0:20).** Establish the synthetic nine-system portfolio and management KPIs.
2. **AI Inventory (0:20–0:35).** Locate `AI-001`, its accountable owners, lifecycle stage and review obligations.
3. **Risk Classification (0:35–1:05).** Show the calculated CRITICAL tier, score and triggered rationale. Explain that the calculation is retained.
4. **Optional override (1:05–1:25).** Propose a tier with rationale. Show that `PROPOSED` does not change the effective tier; approve with a distinct role and show downstream treatment change. For AI-001, retain CRITICAL if no override is needed.
5. **Risk Assessment (1:25–2:05).** Complete the 21-question assessment. Answer `NO` to effective human override to create a HIGH finding and gate implication; then complete an approved assessment with appropriate evidence.
6. **Findings & Exceptions (2:05–2:20).** Show the assessment finding, owner, remediation, due date and lifecycle impact.
7. **Control Requirements (2:20–2:55).** Show controls derived from the effective tier. Evidence `CTRL-009` Human Oversight and selected material controls; demonstrate that a failed or missing control remains visible.
8. **Human Oversight (2:55–3:10).** Show human-in-the-loop review, competence, override, stop authority, escalation and retained evidence.
9. **Lifecycle & Approval (3:10–3:45).** First show `BLOCKED` with missing validation/control evidence. Save independent validation and evidence controls. For `VALIDATED WITH CONDITIONS`, accept and track the conditions. Show the tier-derived Executive Risk Committee authority and record the governance decision.
10. **Monitoring (3:45–4:15).** Trigger the AI-001 RED scenario. Show breached metrics and the explicit governance response.
11. **Executive Overview / Findings (4:15–4:35).** Confirm that the same critical monitoring finding, RED status and revalidation requirement appear across management and remediation views.
12. **Lifecycle & Approval (4:35–4:45).** Show `EVENT-DRIVEN · REVALIDATION REQUIRED` and its specific triggers. Explain that revalidation does not predetermine failure or suspension.
13. **Audit Trail (4:45–5:00).** Finish with the single hash-chained event sequence and `VALID` integrity result.

The demonstration proves the operating chain: intake → inventory → calculated classification → expert challenge → assessment → controls → validation → approval → monitoring → finding → revalidation → evidence.
